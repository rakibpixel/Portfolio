The toolkit

15 tools. One tiny bar.

Every tool is a single click. Tap any card to see exactly what it does and how to use it.

The panel is a vertical strip of icons — hover any icon to see its name and shortcut, then click to run. Some tools open a small floating window for extra options; others run instantly.

Shift & Alt shortcuts. A few icons do more than one thing — a normal click does one, Shift or Alt + click does another. Watch the tooltip; it tells you each mode.
Reorder the bar. Drag any icon up or down to rearrange the toolbar however you like. Your order is saved automatically and remembered next time Photoshop opens.
Getting started

Up and running in minutes

Install once, activate with your key, and the toolbar is ready every time you open Photoshop.

01

Install the extension

Unzip the package and run the installer — RP-Bar drops straight into Photoshop's extensions.

02

Open the panel

In Photoshop go to Window → Extensions → RP-Bar. The toolbar docks to your workspace.

03

Activate your key

Enter the license key you received. It's saved on your machine — you only do this once.

04

Start clicking

Every tool is one tap away. Drag to reorder them however your workflow likes.
